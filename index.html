Local events with a big heart.

Class Vibe Events is all about creating welcoming, memorable occasions for our local communities across Northumberland and the North East. Whether it’s a fundraiser, a themed night, a family-friendly get-together or a private celebration, we want everyone to feel part of it.

We believe in supporting local people, local venues and good causes — while making every event a proper good time.
